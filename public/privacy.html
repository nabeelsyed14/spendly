Spendly Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Spendly is an offline personal budget tracker. This policy explains how the Android app handles information.

Data storage and sharing

Spendly does not operate an account system, server, analytics service, advertising service, or cloud sync. Your transactions, budgets, goals, preferences, imported CSV data, and any data derived from bank messages stay on your device. Spendly does not collect, transmit, sell, or share this data with us or third parties.

Bank SMS tracking

If you explicitly enable Bank SMS Tracking, Spendly requests Android's SMS permission to read messages in your inbox that may describe a bank transaction. It uses matching words and an on-device parser to create a suggested transaction in the Review screen. You decide whether to approve or discard each suggestion. SMS content is never uploaded or shared.

SMS tracking is optional. You can use manual entry, CSV import, and pasted messages without granting SMS access. You can disable tracking in Spendly or revoke the SMS permission in Android Settings at any time.

Notification access

If you explicitly enable notification access in Android Settings, Spendly reads transaction notifications from supported payment and banking apps to create reviewable suggestions. Notification content is processed and stored only on your device and is never uploaded or shared. You can disable this access at any time in Android Settings.

Imported and exported data

CSV files you import are read on your device. Exports are written to your device's Downloads folder. Spendly does not receive either file.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete it in Spendly or clear/uninstall the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling Spendly deletes locally stored app data. Because Spendly has no server copy, deleted data cannot be recovered by us.

Children

Spendly is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. For privacy questions, contact the support email shown on Spendly's Google Play listing.
